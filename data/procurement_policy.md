# Procurement policy, purchase order review

Effective 2026-01-01. Applies to all indirect and capital purchase orders.

## 1. Vendor standing

1.1 A purchase order may only be issued to a vendor with status `approved` or
`probationary` in the vendor registry.

1.2 A vendor with status `unapproved` may not receive a purchase order under any
circumstance, regardless of amount or urgency.

1.3 A vendor on `probationary` status may receive purchase orders up to
25,000 USD. Anything above that requires category manager review.

1.4 Every vendor must have a verified tax identification and a contract on file
before a purchase order is issued.

## 2. Documentation

2.1 A purchase order must name a specific requester. Requests attributed only to
a department or team are incomplete.

2.2 Capital purchases above 50,000 USD must reference a capital project code.

2.3 Line items must be itemized. A single lump sum with no breakdown is
acceptable only below 2,500 USD.

## 3. Competitive sourcing

3.1 Purchases above 75,000 USD require evidence of at least two competing
quotes, unless the vendor is a sole source under an active master agreement.

3.2 Expedited or emergency freight above 10,000 USD requires a written
justification of the urgency.

## 4. Split purchase prevention

4.1 Multiple purchase orders to the same vendor within a 30 day window that
together exceed an approval threshold are treated as a single purchase for
approval purposes.

4.2 A purchase order that closely duplicates a recent order to the same vendor
must be reconciled before issue.

## 5. Category restrictions

5.1 Consulting and professional services require a statement of work, not a
purchase order line item alone.

5.2 Refrigerated logistics vendors must hold current cold chain audit
documentation.
